# NBA Constitution and By-Laws

`nba-constitution-2024.pdf` is not committed — the repo does not redistribute it.
88 pages, ~149k characters, with an 81-entry bookmark outline.

## Why it is here at all

It is a **different document from the CBA**, with different force. The CBA is the
agreement between the league and the players' union; the Constitution and By-Laws
are rules the Members agreed among themselves. The CBA says so directly (p. 322):

> nothing contained in this Agreement shall be deemed to be an agreement of the
> Players Association to any provision of the NBA Constitution and By-Laws

Two rules the engine needs live here rather than in the CBA:

| By-Law | Rule |
|---|---|
| **7.03**, p. 85 | The consecutive-first-round-picks rule, commonly called "Stepien" — unnamed in the text. Also a flat bar on selling first-round pick rights for cash. |
| **4.01(a)**, p. 69 | Trading dates: the deadline is 3 p.m. eastern on the **second Thursday prior to that Season's All-Star Game**, and trading is closed from then until the day after the last Regular Season Game. |

Citations from this document use `engine.constitution.ByLaw`, deliberately
separate from `engine.citations.Citation`, so a verdict can say which document
it rests on. Implying the CBA prohibits something it does not would undermine
the one thing the citations are for.

## What is NOT here

The commonly cited limit on trading picks more than seven Drafts ahead is in
**neither** document. By-Law 7.05 permits the Board of Governors to adopt
additional Draft rules, which is the likely home; those are not published in
either source. Recorded as unsourced rather than guessed at.
